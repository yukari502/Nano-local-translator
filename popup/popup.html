Welcome!

This is an open-source, fast translator based on Chrome Built-in AI.

All translations are processed locally on your device for complete privacy.

Important: First-time use requires downloading translation packs. The first translation may be slightly slow.

🔒 Privacy & Provider Notice

Local Models (Recommended): Using local models (Ollama, LM Studio) processes all translations on your device with 100% privacy.

External Providers: If using third-party cloud endpoints, translated page text is sent to their servers. Ensure you trust the provider.
